BountiesTermsAcceptable Use

What we collect

We process the wallet address you connect; signed authentication messages, nonce and session records; selected roles; bounty scopes and milestones; proposals; evidence URIs and hashes; token inspection results; ratings and reviews; reports and moderation records; notifications; escrow transaction references and canonical contract state; and technical records such as timestamps, browser and network metadata, security events, and service logs. We do not ask for a password, seed phrase, or private key.

Public blockchain data

Wallet addresses, contract calls, token movements, event logs, and commitments on a public blockchain are public and may be permanent. We cannot erase or change chain data. A wallet address is pseudonymous, not necessarily anonymous, and may be linked to other activity by third parties.

Why we use information

We use information to authenticate wallets; operate bounty, proposal, evidence, notification, review, reporting, and moderation features; verify canonical chain state; secure and debug the service; prevent abuse; comply with law; and improve reliability. Final lawful bases and any consent flows depend on the user’s region and must be confirmed before launch.

Infrastructure and recipients

The application is served through Vercel and uses managed Supabase/Postgres and Supabase Functions for persistence and application logic. Wallets, RPC providers, block explorers, blockchain networks, and linked storage may receive transaction or request data. We may disclose information to service providers, professional advisers, transaction participants where the product requires it, and authorities when legally required. A final subprocessor list will be published before launch.

Visibility

Public wallet profiles, marketplace listings, provider proposals accepted into a transaction, evidence links, ratings, reviews, wallet identifiers, token information, and chain references may be visible to other users or the public. Staff can see reported and hidden content needed for moderation. App moderation hides content from ordinary frontend views but does not erase public chain data or records that must be retained for security or legal reasons.

Cookies and local storage

We use security-critical session cookies and browser session storage for authentication and cross-site-request-forgery protection. The production service should not use advertising cookies unless this notice and any required consent mechanism are updated first.

Retention and security

We use access controls, row-level database policies, wallet signatures, short-lived sessions, security logging, and transport protections. No system is perfectly secure. A formal schedule will set how long offchain accounts, content, reports, reviews, notifications, and logs are retained. Onchain data is controlled by the relevant blockchain, not by Bounties.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object, port, or complain about offchain personal data. Identity verification may require a new wallet signature. Rights may be limited by public-chain immutability, legal obligations, fraud prevention, security, or the rights of others. A verified privacy contact and request procedure will be added before launch.

International users and minors

Infrastructure and blockchains may process data across borders. Final transfer safeguards and regional supplements must be documented before launch. Bounties is not directed to children, and users must meet the age and capacity rules in the final Terms.

Changes

We will date material changes and provide notice where required. The final notice will identify the operating entity and privacy contact.
